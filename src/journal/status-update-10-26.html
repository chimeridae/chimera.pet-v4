Enough ENOUGH!!! of being sad. ONLY HAPPY TIMES NOW. Just kidding (sort of). Somewhere along the way, I forgot a journal is meant to be a casual place to write out your thoughts, and not this huge... like, grand announcement. Another month of Hey guys guess what I'm doing bad, yep, still doing bad. Except this time I'm not! Sort of. Well, I'll have to address the elephant in the room: I haven't updated the site in months, and I disappeared from online spaces entirely for almost this entire year. Because I was doing bad..... basically. (It's a lot more complicated than that, but that's all you really need to know.) I should really start talking here more so that every single time I post isn't this huge dreadful announcement that my Life is Hard Right Now or it Isn't. I guess it's because I've felt on the very edge of staying alive for so long, that announcing that I am living at all feels like a big thing. ... Damn, that ended up sounding a little more poetic than I meant for it to.

Well, this isn't intended to be a sappy personal entry (I'll make that one later). I'm writing about some changes I've made to the site since getting medicated, and the ones I plan to do in the future. I'm thinking of turning this into an occasional series of "status updates" as I work on various parts of the site. My .gitignore page gets longer and longer as I work on various things that I'm not ready for the public to see quite yet, but I'm still proud of, so it's kind of like being stuck in limbo... hopefully, since being medicated, this will be a less common occurence!

Things I've already gotten done:

  1. implemented pagination for the journal page
  2. switched the font months ago for better legibility
  3. added a 'sort by author' section, to improve journal usability for myself and others
  4. finished the boxes section that had been in limbo for way too long
  5. made huge headway in terms of pokemon pages, porting over some old articles and adding mobile navigation

  6. installed 11ty's RSS plugin, making the feed generation automated (hello to anyone viewing from there!)

Upcoming things to do:

  1. backport old journal entries? decided not to do this, others can repost theirs at their own pace
  2. bring my cassettes to work to scan their j-cards
  3. add comments using ayano's comment widget
  4. a photos page (from my dsi or otherwise)
  5. port over my hgss page (at least the party), and learn how to use pokeAPI for the dex pages
  6. maybe, just maybe, a page for vix's emerald playthrough. this is a big maybe

My dreams for this website were far more lofty than I could have achieved given the events in my personal life that would go on shortly after I revamped it. Not that I had any way to know that, of course. I'm having to tone down my expectations, but keeping a journal like this ensures no ideas are lost along the way. I struggle a lot with the idea that I'm somehow failing, despite still actively working on something everyday since being medicated the past month (I know that's only been a week). Trying my best to give myself some credit where credit's due.
