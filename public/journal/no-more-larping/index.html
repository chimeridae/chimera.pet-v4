No more LARPing, or whatever

(source)

You know, I hate this whole 'pretending I'm writing as just one person' thing that's going on in our journal section. I think I want it to stop. I find it stupid and restrictive towards my actual identity, and the complexities therewithin. Or whatever. I don't know how to talk as 'cohesively' as "I" (?) have been in these other entries, especially after the events of the past year. Multiplicity has been a severe hinderance to my recovery (who woulda thought), and for a long time, certain parts sent us into complete denial, again. It's hard for me to understand, since I personally have kind of 'always known' about it... to some degree, anyway. I'm still finding out a lot of new things about my identity, new and old, but I've been around in some form since 2009. Within the past few years we reconnected with our childhood best friend, who told us that they remembered ME specifically, my name and everything! That I was 'my imaginary friend'. It was a really sobering experience!

Anyways, Hi Chimera.pet! I'm Fen or Ava (she/they), and I'm just a little fox that likes to type on the computer. I used to run rampant back on xat chatrooms and now I guess I have a website instead. Those of you who have followed our website from the beginning back on Neocities may remember that I actually used to have my own page back in 2023 or so. As you can see, I had a lot more of an edgy demeanor back then compared to now. Lots of internal happenings have resulted in that softening quite a lot (though it still exists elsewhere, just in other parts now).

Personal events led me to shying away from ever using this much... After a certain point I wanted no one to be able to see me, becuase I thought I was some evil bitch. I feel sad that I ever felt that way now. Honestly, the internal split that happened after this results in me not feeling a lot of connection to this old site anyways, even though it was technically "mine"... I feel a lot more connection to my old fennekin collection page, with the bright softer pinks. It feels a lot more like it was (or still is) really mine! I'm excited to get to remaking this page, but we still haven't unpacked from moving at the beginning of this year, oops...

I think the pinks we've used here for these journal entries and other parts of the site are a lot closer to what I like now. Pink has always been my favorite color, since I was a little kit. Oh, by the way, if you noticed that wording in our other entries, that comes from me specifically. I just think it's cute, but everyone else tends to refer to it a little more normally. Anyways, I find it hard to squeeze in any ways of expressing myself in the way our site is set up right now. I don't like to pretend to be a singular collective person all the time, when it couldn't be further from my lived experience. Although it's definitely the "safer" option, it lends itself to denial at the drop of a hat. I don't like that for myself or the other parts of me who I care about protecting. So I'm going to be the one to rip the band-aid off and get this ball rolling (as tends to be the case!). I hope you all won't mind the bit of inconsistency from this point forward; but it's for the better in terms of our health. Wait! Actually, it doesn't matter if you mind or not, it's my website! I get to do whatever I want! >:P

I'm not sure what all I'll talk about here... maybe some therapy talk, some games, some of the site dev experience (I'm trying to co-write a site update alongside this), or just talk about fun little adventures. Lately, I've been trying to go on adventures with my partner to try and help her ease out of her shell a bit. By 'adventures' I really mean just going to stores or trails and stuff, so it's not really as grand as it sounds... but for someone who's been living in a routine of just going to work and sleeping for a year, this is a big thing for us! I really enjoy going to the mall and looking around, just to get out and see the world... they really need to bring back The Mall as part of culture... Thrift stores are great too! We're still stuck at home with our family, but I believe that in order to get out of here we need to build a space that feels comfortable and not stuck in constant limbo. So I've really come to enjoy my little outings. I firmly believe that there is a lot to look forward to in life, and if I was given this life after having it almost ripped away so many times, I want to grab it by the horns and take it! I guess that's another thing that bothered me; all our latest entries are so moody. There's plenty to love in life even when it's hard to live.

I'd like to use this to practice art, too, but I'm still kind of shy about it. Art became so hard for me for so many months, and I don't know how I feel about posting it yet... especially since we're still working on refunding people (I swear we haven't forgotten you). But I want to exist and express myself outside of my 'purpose' so to speak... the reasons why I split, reasons why I front. They're important, but I think anyone would be depressed by a life that's only defined by the sad parts of it, you know? I want to be allowed to have my own hobbies and interests again, even just enjoying some of the same things in 'my own way'.
